# Project Statement

## Project Title

Grocery Store Management

## Problem Statement

In a small grocery shop, the shopkeeper has to keep track of products, prices and available stock. Preparing bills manually can also take time and can result in calculation mistakes.

This project provides a simple Python program where products can be managed, items can be added to a customer's cart and a bill can be generated automatically.

## Scope

The project is mainly designed for a small grocery store.

It includes:
- Adding and removing products
- Updating price and stock
- Searching for products
- Managing a customer's cart
- Generating a bill
- Updating stock after a sale
- Checking stock information

The project does not include online payments, customer accounts or a database.

## Target Users

- Small shop owners
- Shopkeepers
- Store staff

## Main Features

1. Product management
2. Customer cart
3. Billing
4. Stock report
5. Product searching
6. Product sorting
